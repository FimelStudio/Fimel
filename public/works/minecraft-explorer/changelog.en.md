# Changelog

## 1.0.2 · 2026-10-13

- Removed obsolete development notes and persistent logs from Settings, and simplified repeated footers.
- Scan issues are available on demand from the status bar, with language switching, duplicate merging and clearing.
- Launcher states now use readable action hints and correctly show linked launch scripts.

## 1.0.1 · 2026-10-12

- Detect matching last-launch scripts automatically in the PCL2 / PCL CE mapping dialog.
- Require exact version and game-directory matches, and save a separate copy to prevent later launches replacing a mapping.
- Add Auto-detect retries and missing-script guidance while preserving manual entries.

## 1.0.0 · 2026-10-12

- Multi-source content indexing, search, ownership, icons, favorites and tags.
- Storage treemap and category charts with drill-down and automatic analysis of newly added directories.
- Preview-confirmed rename, verified copy/move, backup/restore and recycling.
- PCL2, PCL CE and HMCL opening, plus per-version launch script mapping.
- Immediate, persistent Chinese/English and dark/light switching.
